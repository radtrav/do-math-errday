🏆 Trophy № 1 · Classical Probability
P(A) = |A||Ω|
Favorable outcomes over total outcomes — only when all outcomes are equally likely. In code: A.size / Ω.size. Earned in Lesson 1: The Type System of Uncertainty.
🏆 Trophy № 2 · Conditional Probability
P(A | B) = P(A ∩ B) P(B)
The probability of A given B occurred — filter Ω to B, measure A inside it. In code: Ω.filter(B).filter(A).length / Ω.filter(B).length. Rearranged: P(A∩B) = P(A|B) × P(B) — the multiplication rule. Earned in Lesson 2: The Filter of Belief.
